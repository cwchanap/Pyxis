# Gameplay audio licensing

This directory keeps the legal and provenance evidence for the CAF resources in `Pyxis/Resources/Audio/Gameplay/` available offline.

- `CC0-1.0.txt` is the complete CC0 1.0 Universal legal code, retrieved from the Creative Commons legal-code endpoint.
- `Kenney-RPG-Audio-LICENSE.txt` is the provenance notice bundled inside the official Kenney RPG Audio archive used for all 13 assets.

`docs/audio-assets.md` maps every bundled resource to its original Kenney filename, the processing performed, its measured final duration, and this local license evidence. The original pack notice says that the assets may be used in personal and commercial projects; CC0 permits the documented conversion, modification, and binary-app redistribution.
