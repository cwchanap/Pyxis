# HPA-811 — Effective Drill Yard and attack-gear gains: design specification

**Linear:** https://linear.app/cwchanap/issue/HPA-811/early-drill-yard-and-attack-gear-give-no-damage-gain  
**Baseline:** `cwchanap/Pyxis` `main@98c9d358028d04ec8809a9e3efa5799aa1297422` (UAT baseline, 2026-11-07).  
**Delivery:** **One HPA-811 PR**. This doc and the [implementation plan](../plans/2026-11-07-hpa-811-attack-upgrade-rounding-implementation.md) are its first commits; implementation, tests, balance evidence and review fixes belong on the **same PR**. Do not merge planning docs alone.

## Problem

At low integer attack power, buying Drill Yard (+5% per level) or equipping attack gear (+15% at Lv1, +22.5% at Lv2, +30% at Lv3) often adds **zero** damage after rounding. UAT observed a 16-gold Drill Yard upgrade showing `2 → 2 attack (no damage change yet)` and even leveled attack gear showing `(no gain yet)`. An honest warning still makes the purchase/reward unsatisfying.

On baseline `main@98c9d35`, the first-round formula was `round(baseSoldierAttack × cityDefenseTrait × (1 + 0.05×DrillYard) × attackGearMultiplier × Renown)`, minimum 1. `KingdomGameState.traitAdjustedSoldierAttackPower(for:level:)` provided the state-aware projection for live spawns and Kingdom previews; the idle path instead used the static percentage primitive with a cached multiplier, and the test-only campaign equipment scorer reimplemented the first round. **As implemented on this PR**, the instance `traitAdjustedSoldierAttackPower(for:level:)` is the shared **effective** projection for live spawns, idle damage and Kingdom previews — it delegates to the bounded `effectiveSoldierAttackPower` recurrence, whose raw cells still compose through the one static `firstRoundAttackMultiplier(drillYard:attackFraction:renown:)` — and the corrected scorer evaluates candidates on a copied state through that same instance seam **after** Treasury and Camp purchases.

## Product decision and power tradeoff

**The first bought Drill Yard rank and the first equipped attack-gear rank must each add at least +1 integer *first-round* ordinary-soldier attack**, even when the authored percentages round away. Later ranks must never *reduce* attack (the result is nondecreasing in each rank dimension) but may legitimately round away. When the original rounded percentage produces a larger result, keep it. Do not simply add the ranks to every percentage result.

**History — rejected stronger rule:** the original decision guaranteed +1 at *every* rank of each dimension (`effective(d,g) = max(raw, effective(d−1,g)+1, effective(d,g−1)+1)`). It was implemented at `5730d9a`, measured on the mandatory campaign gate below, and **failed the >35%-faster guardrail** (fresh boughtGear 57.7%, bought 55.8%, freeSeeded 60.5% and replay Renown 0 57.6% of their matched controls — see *Measured gate evidence*). The user chose to revise the rule rather than override the guardrail; the rejected run's raw trace is preserved under `evidence/hpa-811/` for history.

This is a **bounded low-power nudge**, not an every-rank buff. At a base attack of 1, the first Drill Yard rank doubles attack (1 → 2, +100% incremental), and the combined first-rank edges can at most triple the rank-0 rounded result — the level-1 first-run maximum is **3×** the un-upgraded value (DY4 + gear Lv3 reaches 3 vs 1). The floor adds at most +2 over a cell's own raw value and fades as base attack grows and the percentages dominate on their own.

Examples with the current soldier curve `ceil(1.38^(level−1))`, a **neutral city trait and Renown 0** (these are calculated projections, **not measured campaign times**):

| Soldier level (base) | Drill Yard 4 + Attack gear Lv1: raw → effective | Drill Yard 4 + Attack gear Lv3: raw → effective |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 (1) | 1 → **3** (3×) | 2 → **3** |
| 2–3 (2) | 3 → **4** | 3 → **4** |
| 4 (3) | 4 → **5** | **5 → 5** |
| 5 (4) | **6 → 6** | **6 → 6** |
| 8 (10) | **14 → 14** | **16 → 16** |
| 10 (19) | **26 → 26** | **30 → 30** |

Lv3 equipment is earned on replay, but this table deliberately holds **Renown at 0** to isolate the rounding rule. An actual Renown 1+ replay will have different raw/effective values and must be measured separately. A high-base example (soldier Lv10, DY4, Lv3 attack gear, neutral, Renown 0) remains `raw == effective == 30`.

**Other consequence:** Attack gear will generally win more early loadout decisions. Specialist and Toughness gear can still round away their own effects and must keep their truthful `(no gain yet)` note; do not silently buff them. This is a product balance tradeoff, not a request for new systems.

## Exact bounded effective-attack rule

At one soldier level, city defense trait and Renown:

- Clamp the target `d` to `0...TreasuryUpgrade.drillYard.maxLevel` (0...4), including direct test mutations of `treasury.drillYard` outside its usual normalized range. Clamp target `g` to `0...3` **only when** the input effect is `.attackBonus` *and* the owned item level is positive. Otherwise `g = 0`, including unequipped/Toughness/Specialist or an `.attackBonus` effect paired with `ownedGearLevel <= 0`. An effect being non-nil does **not** imply that gear is active.
- `raw(d,g)` = the existing static, single-round integer attack using the hypothetical ranks: base attack × defense trait × Renown × `(1 + 0.05×d)` × `(1 + attackFractionAtLevel(g))`. **When `g == 0`, set `attackFraction = 0` directly, even if the unscaled input happens to be `.attackBonus(0.15)`.** Call `EquipmentEffect.scaled(toLevel: g)` **only for `g >= 1`**. The existing helper clamps `scaled(toLevel: 0)` to level 1 and would incorrectly apply +15% to the no-gear baseline. Preserve Swift `.rounded()` and the minimum 1.
- `effective(0,0) = raw(0,0)`.
- `effective(d,g) = max(raw(d,g), effective(d−1,g)+(d==1 ? 1 : 0) if d>0, effective(d,g−1)+(g==1 ? 1 : 0) if g>0)` — nonexistent predecessors are omitted.
- Return `effective(actual d, actual g)` from the state-aware attack method. Compute the tiny ≤5×4 table on demand; **no persisted modifier or separate attack state**.

The two predecessor terms guarantee strict growth only at the **first bought rank** of each dimension (`d==1`, `g==1`), even with the other upgrade present; later ranks are **nondecreasing but not strictly increasing** — they can round away. Important comparisons at neutral Renown 0 and level-1 base power: `(d,g)=(0,0)→1`, `(1,0)→2`, `(4,0)→2`, `(0,1)→2`, `(0,3)→2`, `(1,1)→3`, `(4,3)→3`.

The rejected every-rank recurrence (`+1` at *every* predecessor edge) was pointwise minimal under the original all-ranks guarantee, but its lift compounded with rank count and cut matched campaign totals by 28–44% (see *Measured gate evidence*). The first-rank-only edges above keep the guarantee exactly where the UAT round-away was observed (first purchases) and bound the total lift to +2 over a cell's own raw. The additive variant `raw(d,g)+min(d,1)+min(g,1)` is *also* monotonic, but inflates already-effective high-base percentages by a permanent additive term (e.g. adds 2 to a Lv10 raw attack of 30); the 2-D predecessor recurrence retains the old high-base values whenever raw percentage increases suffice.

## Code ownership, small scope

1. Keep `KingdomGameState` as the single combat-rule owner. Preserve the existing pure static `traitAdjustedSoldierAttackPower` as the **unchanged raw percentage primitive**. Add an **internal static, pure** `effectiveSoldierAttackPower` helper taking soldier type/level, defense trait, Drill Yard rank, the unscaled attack `EquipmentEffect?` and gear level, and Renown multiplier. The test matrix calls it directly, without building thousands of game states. The function must treat `ownedGearLevel <= 0` as no attack gear and must never use `scaled(toLevel: 0)`; add explicit zero/negative/above-cap gear and directly assigned out-of-range Drill Yard tests.
2. Add **one static first-round multiplier composer** for `(drillYard, attackFraction, renown)` to evaluate hypothetical cells. The existing `firstRoundAttackMultiplier(for:)` becomes a thin instance wrapper around it; the existing state-aware `traitAdjustedSoldierAttackPower(for:level:)` calls the static effective helper using existing Treasury/Equipment/Renown state. Do not duplicate the 5% constant or authored gear fractions.
3. **Live and idle parity:** `BattleScene` continues spawning ordinary soldiers using the state-aware method, caching stats once. In idle `applyAbstractBuildingSpawnDamage`, replace the static percentage call and cached `firstRound` factor with that same state-aware method; delete the now-unused local `let defenseTrait = currentCityDefenseTrait`, but retain the specialist snapshot, Guard absorption, role/specialist second round, damage-spill rules and at-most-one-city conquest. Neither Guards nor Vanguard Captain use this projection; do not change them.
4. **Kingdom UI, only after passing the balance gate:** keep the Drill Yard's real current→next attack example **and** its `changeNote` / `(no damage change yet)` branch plus the narrow-row fallback `.replacingOccurrences(of: " (no damage change yet)", with: " (no gain)")` — under the first-rank-only rule those branches are **not** dead: later Drill Yard ranks can still produce no integer gain and must stay visibly and accessibly disclosed. Remove the now-unreachable Attack case's old comparison from `equippedEffectRoundsAway` (the first equipped attack-gear rank always gains), but preserve that helper's Specialist/Toughness truth. In `KingdomScene.armoryRowText(for:state:)`, **append exactly `" (atk \(bareAttack) → \(equippedAttack))"`** when the effect is `.attackBonus`, using a copied unequipped state and the same instance projection; do this *after* the existing pure nominal `equipmentEffectText(for:level:)` formatter, which must remain unchanged and keep `"Attack Lv N · +P%"`. VoiceOver already consumes `armoryRowText.line2`, so no parallel accessibility formatter is needed. Keep non-Attack no-gain disclosures and narrow-row fitting tests. **Do not change `TreasuryUpgrade.drillYard.effectName`** (not rendered in the Drill Yard row); no new layout, tabs, art, sound, or strings platform.
5. **Test-only equipment scorer:** project candidates using a copied state and its real equip/unequip + effective attack method, then existing role/specialist second round. Score at `state.manualSoldierLevel(for: type) ?? 1` **after Treasury and Camp purchases**. Before this PR (`main@98c9d35`), `runGreedyCampaign` chose gear before Camp, so merely replacing the hard-coded level 1 while keeping the old order would still have evaluated level 1. Keep deterministic ties; it is not a runtime recommendation system. To isolate effects, compare against a scorer-corrected **no-new-floor** control as well as unchanged historical main.
6. No new persistence, schema/version layer, migration, general-purpose buff registry, combat engine, feature flag, art/SFX or HPA-812 UI polish. Implementation and adjustments stay on this PR.

## Test acceptance and mandatory early balance gate

- **Pure matrix:** soldier levels 1–19, all five soldier types, neutral/advantaged/disadvantaged traits, Renown multipliers 1/1.25/1.5/2, DY 0–4, attack gear rank 0–3. Assert the **first** rank in each dimension raises effective integer attack ≥1 over its zero-rank predecessor, and every later rank is **nondecreasing** (≥, not strict). Test `.attackBonus(0.15)` passed with `ownedGearLevel = 0` (and negative) still produces the **no-gear baseline**, not Lv1, and that rank >3 and Drill Yard direct mutation >4 clamp. Pin `Lv10 + DY4 + Lv3 == raw == 30` (neutral, Renown 0), the level-1 later-rank ties `(4,0)=2`, `(0,3)=2`, `(4,3)=3`, and `DY0 + no attack gear == old raw` for representative states. Add a few real-state and live/idle integration cases instead of creating thousands of full `KingdomGameState` fixtures.
- **Existing tests to reconcile:** model/scorer updates in `TreasuryEffectSeamTests.swift` (the instance DY4 test, zero-equipment DY0...10 test, live/idle and cached-spawn tests), `EquipmentEffectTests.swift` (attack+DY instance values), and four always-on `Country1SiegeSmokeTests.swift` greedy-policy tests. Reserve `KingdomSceneTests.swift` old no-gain, current/next, max-level, gear, Renown and Armory assertions for the **post-balance UI checkpoint**. Keep the old *static* raw-rounding pins.
- **Reachable Specialist-over-Attack fixture:** City 1 with a seeded Barracks Lv5 (legal starting-city building), first-live **Gate**, and owned `c01-infantry-attack`, `c03-infantry-toughness`, `c06-infantry-gate` (a replay-style owned-gear fixture): attack first-round 5 vs matching Gate-specialist second-round 6. Prefer this to putting a Siege Workshop in City 5, where the Workshop normally stays locked until City 11. For the `scaled(toLevel:)` and Renown scorer tests, use `c06-infantry-gate` against City 1's Gate to retain observable round-away thresholds; do not expect attack Lv1/Lv3 to round away under the new rule.
- **Baseline controls before gameplay-rule changes:** on a macOS/Xcode runner, capture opt-in seed-582 campaign output from unchanged `main@98c9d35` for historical comparison; then, with *production combat math still unchanged*, apply only the test-only **post-Camp, real-level scorer** improvement and rerun it as the matched control. Record all actual `campaign=1`/`elapsed=`/`camp=` and `totalElapsed=` lines for fresh bought+gear and Renown 0/1 replay. Do not invent measurements or compare two different scorer policies as though only the floor changed.
- **Early stop criterion, BEFORE any Kingdom UI edits:** run the same chained campaign with the effective attack rule and corrected scorer. **Stop and seek a product decision** if any variant fails to conquer 15/15 or stalls, if **any City 1–5 of the fresh bought+gear variant completes in under 5.0 simulated seconds**, or if **any matched 15-city total is below 65% of its scorer-corrected control** (>35% faster). Renown 1 must remain faster than Renown 0; explicitly record zero-gold replay City 1/2. These numbers are **provisional game-feel guardrails**, not empirically validated ideal pacing.
- **Final checks:** after the early gate passes, validate the Kingdom projected integers and accessibility, existing cached-live-soldier behavior, unchanged conquest gold/HP/role/specialist semantics, serial Swift Testing/UI tests, Debug/Release build, SwiftLint, and repeat the required opt-in chained campaign at the final commit. Record empirical results in the same PR; do not tune unrelated balance constants or hide a failure.
